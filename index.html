What's Inside

Built Different.

Every KSR filter uses aerospace-grade synthetic media — not paper. The result: a filter that breathes harder, lasts longer, and performs every single ride.

01

Triple-Layer Synthetic Media

Three layers of precision-woven synthetic fibre capture 99% of particulates while allowing 3x the airflow of OEM paper filters.

02

Aerospace-Grade Wire Mesh

Stainless steel inner & outer mesh provides structural rigidity even at redline RPMs. No collapse, no failure.

03

High-Temp Epoxy Seal

End caps bonded with heat-resistant epoxy rated for sustained temperatures up to 200°C in the engine bay.
